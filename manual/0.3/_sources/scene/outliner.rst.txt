########
Outliner
########

The Outliner is Max's Scene Explorer. Collections are layers; the active collection is the
current layer.

* **Type buttons** in the header filter by Geometry, Shapes, Lights, Cameras, Helpers, Bones.
* **Freeze** column (snowflake): frozen objects draw gray and can't be selected.
* **Renderable** column, **See-Through** and **Display as Box** per object.
* **Double-click** selects an object and its children; F2 or Ctrl+click renames.
* **Right-click:** Unhide All, Unfreeze All, Color By Layer (objects take their collection's
  color tag).
