#######
Cameras
#######

* **V** over a viewport opens the Point-of-View menu: the scene's cameras, Perspective,
  Orthographic, the six views, Select Camera, Lock Camera, Field of View and Roll.
* **Lock Camera to View** is on in new files: navigating in a camera view moves the camera
  (Max's Orbit, Truck, Dolly).
* The button strip at the bottom right of a camera view: Dolly, Field of View, Pan, Orbit, Roll.
* **Target Camera** in the Create tab: drag from the camera position to the target.
* **Camera Correction:** two-point perspective through lens shift.
