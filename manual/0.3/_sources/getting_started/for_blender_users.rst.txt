###################
Coming from Blender
###################

Moritz is Blender underneath. Your files, add-ons and Python scripts work, and Blender tutorials
stay usable:

* **Both shortcuts:** menus and tooltips show Blender's key next to Moritz's where they differ.
* **Blender's names:** set **Preferences > Keymap > Names** to Blender, or the keymap itself to
  Blender, to follow a tutorial key for key.
* **F3 search** finds every operator under its Blender name as well.

What is different:

* **Settings** live in their own folder (see :doc:`install`), so Moritz never changes your
  Blender preferences.
* **The Properties editor** is the :doc:`/interface/command_panel`: Create, Modify and Utilities
  come first, then Blender's tabs in their usual order.
* **Right-click** opens the :doc:`/interface/quad_menu`.
* **Middle mouse** always navigates, even with a tool active.
* **Edit Mode** has five levels (Vertex, Edge, Border, Polygon, Element) and the Edit Poly modifier
  lets you edit a mesh in the middle of its modifier stack.
* **Areas dock and stack as tabs** by dragging their headers (see :doc:`/interface/docking`).
