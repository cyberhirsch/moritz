###########
Quick Setup
###########

The first start of Moritz opens the Quick Setup on the splash screen. Change any of it later in
**Preferences**.

Keymap
   **Moritz** (the default), **Max** or **Blender**. See :doc:`/interface/keymaps_and_names`.

Names
   **Follow Keymap** (the default), **Blender**, **Max** or **Moritz**: whether menus and buttons
   say Weld or Merge by Distance, Chamfer or Bevel, TurboSmooth or Subdivision Surface.

Everything else (theme, language, select with left or right mouse) is Blender's own
:blender:`Quick Setup <getting_started/configuration/defaults.html>`.
